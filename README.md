# Henderson–Whetro Family Explorer

The family history of the six Henderson children in one app: an interactive family tree, a migration map from Scotland, Ireland, Germany and France to northwest Ohio, a map of the cemeteries, the family story, the full research report, charts and the research to-do list. It installs to an iPhone home screen and works offline once opened.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Tree | Tap anyone to move back a generation; search by name |
| Map | Journeys from Europe and Quebec to Ohio, line by line, and the cemeteries with directions |
| Story | *The Henderson–Whetro Family Story*, oldest first |
| Report | The full research notes, with sources |
| Charts | Fan chart, bowtie, lines of descent, origins, timelines and more |
| To-do | Record requests and next steps; checkmarks are saved on the device |

The **Book** view (from the Tree tab's menu) and `Henderson-Whetro-Family-Book.pdf` hold every ancestor as a printable book.

## Install on iPhone

Open the site in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Privacy

This public copy leaves out living people. The six children appear as one "Henderson children" entry, their parents as "Their father (living)" and "Their mother (living)", and living aunts and uncles are not listed. A fuller private copy is kept separately.

## Updating

The published page is the single file `index.html`. After replacing it, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` so installed copies update.
